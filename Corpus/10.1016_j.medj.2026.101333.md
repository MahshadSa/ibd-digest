# Clinical readiness of foundation and agentic AI in computational pathology.

DOI: [10.1016/j.medj.2026.101333](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.medj.2026.101333)

## Abstract

Foundation and agentic AI show great promise for computational pathology, with clinical readiness now widely anticipated. We define readiness at the intended-use system level; outline requirements for evidence, validation, economic sustainability, and governance; and propose a roadmap toward responsible deployment.
