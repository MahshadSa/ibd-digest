# Extending health equity in IBD to incarcerated populations

DOI: [10.1093/ibd/izag249](https://doi.org/10.1093/ibd/izag249)

## Abstract

ABSTRACT

Background
Kidney injury during metabolic dysfunction‐associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD) progression remains incompletely characterized.


Purpose
To assess multiparametric MRI (mpMRI) for early detection and monitoring of MASLD‐associated kidney injury.


Study Type
Animal model.


Animal Model

Fifty male Sprague Dawley rats received high‐fat diet plus very low‐dose carbon tetrachloride (CCl
4
) underwent mpMRI before sacrifice at 0, 2, 4, 8, or 12 weeks (
n
= 10/time point). Ten MASLD and ten normal rats underwent serial MRI at each time point; ten CCl
4
‐only rats served as nephrotoxicity controls. Twelve additional healthy rats underwent a scan‐rescan test.



Field Strength/Sequence
3 T; turbo spin‐echo T2‐weighted imaging, variable flip angle (VFA) gradient‐echo T1 mapping, multi‐echo gradient‐echo blood oxygen level‐dependent imaging, pulsed arterial spin labeling, echo‐planar diffusion kurtosis imaging, and multi‐echo Dixon imaging.


Assessment
Two radiologists (5 and 13 years of experience) quantified T1, T2*, proton density fat fraction (PDFF), mean diffusivity, mean kurtosis (MK), and renal blood flow (RBF) in cortex, outer/inner stripes of outer medulla (OSOM/ISOM). Early injury was defined as a tubular injury score ≥ 2. Histopathology, immunohistochemistry, transcriptomics, and serum metabolomics were performed.


Statistical Tests

Repeated‐measures ANOVA; intraclass correlation coefficient (ICC); area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC).
p
< 0.05 indicated significance.



Results

Scan‐rescan ICCs were 0.682–0.876. OSOM MK/PDFF significantly increased by Week 2 [MK (×10
−3
): 0.532 ± 0.007 to 0.574 ± 0.004; PDFF (%): 2.489 ± 0.243 to 3.600 ± 0.347]; cortical RBF and OSOM T2* showed no significant changes at Week 2 (
p
= 0.786, 1.000) but significantly decreased from Week 4. OSOM MK/PDFF had AUCs of 0.952 and 0.905 for early renal injury. Multi‐omics revealed inflammatory and lipid/bile acid alterations. Dodecanedioic/hippuric acids potentially linked MASLD severity to OSOM MK.



Data Conclusion
Renal mpMRI detected early kidney injury during MASLD progression.


Evidence Level
2.


Technical Efficacy
Stage 2.
