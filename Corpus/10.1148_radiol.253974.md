# Call to Action: Accelerating AI-driven Transformation in Medical                     Imaging and the Broader Health Care System

DOI: [10.1148/radiol.253974](https://doi.org/10.1148/radiol.253974)

## Abstract

This consensus statement from the International Society for Strategic Studies in Radiology calls for artificial intelligence–driven strategies to streamline workflows and align radiology with broader health system and payer priorities amid rapid digital innovation.
