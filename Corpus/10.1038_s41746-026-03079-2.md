# An explainable generative AI system for video-to-report generation in capsule endoscopy

DOI: [10.1038/s41746-026-03079-2](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41746-026-03079-2)

## Abstract

Abstract
Gastrointestinal (GI) diseases remain a significant global health burden due to elevated morbidity and mortality. Accurate and timely diagnosis, especially for small-bowel conditions, remains challenging because capsule endoscopy (CE) videos last 6–12 hours and require prolonged manual review. While artificial intelligence has advanced CE analysis, integrated video-to-report generation remains largely unexplored. We introduce CE Reporter, an explainable system transforming full-length CE videos into evidence-supported diagnostic reports through multi-task keyframe detection and time-aware vision-language alignment. CE Reporter was trained on MICS-CE, a multicenter, multi-device dataset comprising 1002 video-report pairs, 109 million frames, 11,028 video hours, and 29,338 expert-annotated keyframe-text pairs. The model performed strongly across three dimensions: (1) Report quality: exceeding existing multimodal large language models in linguistic coherence and diagnostic agreement (BLEU-ave: 0.572 vs. 0.523; small intestine lesion F1-score: 0.79 vs. 0.58); (2) Clinician evaluation: achieving high correctness, completeness, and conciseness ratings, outperforming resident clinicians while reducing reporting time by 91.9%; (3) Clinical utility: generating explainable reports supported by visual keyframes and maintaining competitive performance across centers and capsule devices (F1 score: 0.72–0.81). These findings establish a foundation for automated CE video-to-report generation and highlight its potential for efficient and transparent clinical workflows.
