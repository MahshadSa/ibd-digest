# Evaluation of Super-Resolution Contrast-enhanced US in Crohn Disease                     Activity Assessment

DOI: [10.1148/radiol.253558](https://doi.org/10.1148/radiol.253558)

## Abstract

In participants with Crohn disease (CD), intestinal super-resolution contrast-enhanced US demonstrated excellent performance in differentiating CD activity through high-resolution depiction of microstructural changes, with superior performance to conventional contrast-enhanced US.
