# Fecal calprotectin changes after polyethylene glycol bowel preparation in patients with ulcerative colitis in remission

DOI: [10.1093/ibd/izag193](https://doi.org/10.1093/ibd/izag193)

## Abstract

Abstract
This prospective paired-sample study evaluated fecal calprotectin changes after polyethylene glycol-based bowel preparation in ulcerative colitis patients in remission. Calprotectin increased modestly, particularly in patients with Mayo 1 endoscopic subscore, but clinical worsening was uncommon and healthy individuals showed no significant change.
