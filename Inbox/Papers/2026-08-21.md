  Nearest seed: Clinical Application of Intestinal Ultrasound in Inflammatory Bowel Disease.

  > [!abstract]-
  > This review examines the recent increase in both clinical and radiological interest in ultrasound in inflammatory bowel disease (IBD), with particular emphasis on its role in the assessment of Crohn's disease. Intestinal ultrasound (IUS) has emerged as an attractive imaging modality because of its non-invasive nature, safety, repeatability, low cost, and ability to provide real-time assessment of IBD. In this work, we develop a series of lateral flow immunoassays for the detection of IBD biomarkers lactoferrin and myeloperoxidase, in an artificial stool matrix.
