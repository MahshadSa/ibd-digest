  > BACKGROUND: This study aimed to investigate the clinical features, treatment responses, and outcomes of pediatric patients with Crohn's disease (CD) and perianal disease (PD) in China, with a particular focus on age at diagnosis. METHODS: We retrospectively analyzed 144 children diagnosed with CD between 2015 and 2024.
